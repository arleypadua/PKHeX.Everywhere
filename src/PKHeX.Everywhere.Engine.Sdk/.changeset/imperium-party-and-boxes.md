With `game.enableFormat("emerald-imperium")`, an Emerald Imperium 1.x save loads. Its party and 14 boxes list, a Pokémon's level, nature, moves, held item, nickname and ball can be edited, and export keeps every byte it didn't change. Imperium's own forms show by name and are read-only. Its own moves and items show as Unknown.
