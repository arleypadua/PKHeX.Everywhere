Colosseum and XD now trade with Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen by `['link']`, both ways. `TransferRefusal` adds `shadow` for a shadow Pokémon, which the games won't trade. Moving a Pokémon into or out of a Box Ruby & Sapphire save no longer evolves it or resets its friendship.
