`game.get()` returns `statsApproximate`, which is true when computed stats may differ from the game's, as in ROM hacks. Unbound and Radical Red now only offer what their saves can store: their own species, items, moves and 26 balls, and Gen 3 languages and origin games. Both lock `nature`, `ability` and `gender`. Radical Red names met locations with FireRed's places. Unbound shows them as `Location #n` and locks `metLocation`.
