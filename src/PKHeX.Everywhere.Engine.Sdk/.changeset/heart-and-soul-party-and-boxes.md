With `game.enableFormat("heart-and-soul")`, a Pokémon Heart & Soul 2.0 save loads. Its party and 14 boxes list, a Pokémon's level, nature, moves, held item, nickname and ball can be edited, and export keeps every byte it didn't change. Heart & Soul's own items show as Unknown. The trainer's money and the bag aren't read yet.
