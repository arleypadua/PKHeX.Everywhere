Unofficial transfers through Generations 1 and 2 keep the Pokémon's identity. A Pokémon moved into Gen 1 or 2 keeps its original trainer and, if it has no nickname, gets the game's default name. A Pokémon moved up from Gen 1 or 2 into Gen 3 or later keeps its language, gender and shininess, and arrives with no EVs. Its PID is no longer 0. The same Pokémon always gets the same PID, so Pokémon from one trainer no longer share an `identityKey`.
